Canada has government-registered account types, such as the TFSA, RRSP and FHSA, that come with tax rules. Each has its own purpose, contribution room and withdrawal rules.

- Rules and limits change, so always check the current figures with the Canada Revenue Agency.
- This overview is general education, not tax advice.
