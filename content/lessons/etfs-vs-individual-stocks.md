An exchange-traded fund (ETF) is a basket of many investments that trades like a single share. An individual stock is a share in one company.

- A single ETF can hold hundreds of companies, so it is more spread out than one stock.
- ETFs have fees, usually expressed as a percentage.
- This lesson explains the difference. It does not recommend any product.
