An emergency fund is money set aside for surprises such as a job gap or urgent repair, kept somewhere easy to reach and not subject to market swings.

- Many people build this cushion before investing so they are not forced to sell at a bad time.
- How much is right depends on your situation. A registered professional can help you decide.
