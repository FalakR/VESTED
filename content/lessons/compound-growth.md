Compounding means growth is earned on your starting amount and on earlier growth. Over long periods the effect builds, which is why time and consistency matter in the Future You simulator.

- Money that grows can itself grow in later periods.
- Starting earlier or contributing steadily changes the picture more than most people expect.
- Real investments fluctuate. The simulator uses a fixed assumed rate only to illustrate the idea.

Try it: change the number of years in Future You and watch the modeled growth.
