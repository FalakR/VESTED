Risk is the chance that an investment's value differs from what you hoped, including falling. Diversification means spreading money across different kinds of investments so one poor result matters less.

- Higher potential growth usually comes with more ups and downs.
- Diversification reduces some risks but never removes all risk.
- Your timeline and comfort with swings matter.

No outcome is guaranteed.
